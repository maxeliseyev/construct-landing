This link is out of date

Konstruct no longer accepts contact links of this form. Ask the person who sent it for a fresh invite — a QR code or a link from the app — and open that one instead.

Why it stopped working

A link like this one carried an account identifier and nothing else. It was not signed, so anyone who knew an identifier could produce one, and being sent a link was enough to be added — which is the opposite of what an invite is supposed to mean here.

Invites are signed now. Each one can be accepted once, stops working twelve hours after it was created, and a link that has already been used does nothing at all. That is what makes starting a conversation something both people agreed to rather than something one of them could arrange alone.

The app says the same thing when it sees one of these links: This link format is no longer supported. Ask for a fresh invite QR or link. Nothing is wrong with your app or your account.

Getting a working invite

Ask your contact to share a new invite from the app, then open the link they send or scan the QR code. If you do not have Konstruct yet, it is in beta on iOS — join the TestFlight beta first, then open the new invite.
